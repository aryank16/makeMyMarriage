Questions

Do guests need to create an account?

No. Never. They get a link, they open it, they reply.

Can my family help manage it?

Yes. Everyone sees the same guest list, tasks and expenses.

Who can see our photos?

Only people with the gallery link. It isn’t listed anywhere or indexed by search engines.

We haven’t fixed the date yet.

That’s fine. Add your functions now and fill in dates later.

Is this for wedding planners?

No. It’s built for one wedding — yours.

What happens to our data afterwards?

It stays. Download everything or delete all of it, whenever you want.
